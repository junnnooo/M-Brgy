Resident user guide

A quick guide to using the kaBRGY community services portal.

01

Read public updates

Check the notice board on the home page, or open Announcements, to read community news, schedules, and emergency alerts.

02

Create your account

On the login page, choose Resident account and sign up with your name, email, and a password. Then open My Account to add your address and contact details.

03

Verify in person

Visit the barangay hall with a valid ID or proof of address. Only verified accounts can request documents or book appointments.

04

Request a document

Choose a document, explain its purpose, and select a preferred date and time. Check your request status and wait for pickup notification.

05

Report and track concerns

Submit the concern category, location, and description. Keep the reference number and use Track a Report to follow its progress.
