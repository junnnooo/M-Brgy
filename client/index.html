About kaBRGY

kaBRGY is a community-centered website for residents who need faster and more efficient action from their barangay officials. Instead of going to the barangay hall for every transaction, residents can request documents, schedule appointments, report community concerns, and receive announcements and emergency alerts online. It also gives barangay officials and admins one place to verify residents, process requests, and respond to reports.

Why choose kaBRGY

A safe, secure, and user-oriented platform for all barangay transactions, promoting transparency and accountability.

  • Secure & Private

    Protects residents' personal information and account details.

  • 24/7 Accessibility

    Allows residents to access online services anytime.

  • Transparency

    Keeps residents informed about announcements and the status of their requests.

  • User-Oriented

    Prioritizes residents' needs through a simple and convenient interface.

  • Efficiency

    Reduces unnecessary visits, waiting times, and paperwork.

  • Reliability

    Provides consistent services and dependable information.
